# Sales Enablement

Use approved product, customer and commercial evidence to create reusable sales artifacts. Every claim in a playbook should identify its source or be marked as a hypothesis.

## Artifact rules

- Separate customer language, internal interpretation and proposed wording.
- Keep price, discount and legal commitments behind an explicit approval boundary.
- Prefer one next customer action over a long internal task list.
- Include disqualification and stop rules, not only persuasion tactics.
- Use `sales_apply_artifact` only after the complete Markdown preview is reviewed and `confirm: true` is supplied.

## Non-goals

This skill does not send emails, make calls, create CRM activities, submit proposals, accept terms or promise a close date to a customer.
